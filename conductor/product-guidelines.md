# Product Guidelines - Schooledule

## Prose Style

- **Tone:** Formal and Institutional. Use clear, direct, and authoritative language suitable for educational institutions.
- **Language:** Spanish (based on existing schema/docs) should be the primary language, maintaining a professional and respectful tone at all times.
- **Terminology:** Consistent use of academic terms (e.g., "Matrícula", "Módulo", "Calificación") as defined in the PostgreSQL schema.

## Branding & Visual Identity

- **Style:** Modern Educational. A vibrant and dynamic aesthetic designed to engage students while remaining professional for faculty and administration.
- **Color Palette:** High-contrast combinations that prioritize readability and energy.
- **Typography:** Clean, sans-serif fonts to ensure clarity and modern feel across all devices.

## UX & Design Principles

- **Accessibility First:** Strictly adhere to WCAG 2.1 guidelines to ensure the platform is usable by all individuals, regardless of ability.
- **Efficiency & Speed:** Optimize workflows for frequent tasks like grade entry, schedule checks, and enrollment to minimize user effort.
- **Extreme Simplicity:** Maintain minimalist interfaces that reduce cognitive load, ensuring that even complex administrative tasks feel intuitive.

## Error Handling & Feedback

- **Role-Based Detail:**
  - **Administrators:** Provide detailed error logs and technical context to facilitate rapid troubleshooting.
  - **Teachers/Students:** Deliver concise, non-technical messages that protect system integrity while guiding the user toward a resolution.
- **Consistency:** Use standardized components for all feedback (success, warning, error) to build user trust and familiarity.
